Elixir Sort – Support

Need help or found a bug? Write to mabou798@gmail.com. Please include your device model and the level number.

How do I play?

Tap a bottle, then tap another bottle to pour. You can pour onto the same color or into an empty bottle. Fill every bottle with a single color to win.

I bought "Remove Ads" on another device

Open Settings in the game and tap "Restore purchases" with the same Apple ID or Google account.

A purchase did not arrive

Close and reopen the game: pending purchases are delivered on start. If it is still missing, contact us. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

My progress is gone

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the game deletes it.
