Elixir Sort – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Elixir Sort: Potion Puzzle ("the game") is made by TerraVision ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why. We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not sell personal data.

1. Data stored only on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives, settings and purchased items) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. Deleting the game deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android),
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • information about ads you see and tap, and basic device and crash information.

On iPhone, the game asks for your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before any tracking. If you decline, you still get ads, but they are not personalised. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in your device settings.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase succeeded, so it can deliver the item and restore it later.

4. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the game schedules at most one local notification a day on your device. No data is sent to us for this.

6. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls. You can reset your advertising identifier and limit ad tracking in your device settings.

8. Changes

We will update this page if the game starts using data in a new way. The date at the top shows the latest version.
